# Spendova Brand Foundation

## 1. Working brand name

**Spendova**

Pronunciation: **Spend-oh-va**

Status: selected working brand, pending formal trademark, domain, app-store, and social-handle clearance.

## 2. Meaning

Spendova combines:

- **Spend** — the everyday movement and use of money;
- **Nova** — a new perspective, new beginning, and visible source of light.

In this product, “spend” does not mean expenses alone. It represents the complete movement of personal money:

- money earned;
- money spent;
- money transferred;
- money borrowed and repaid;
- money saved;
- money invested;
- money reserved for future goals.

Spendova turns those movements into a clear picture so the user can understand the present and plan what comes next.

## 3. Brand promise

**Spendova helps people see where their money moves, understand what it means, and plan their next decision with confidence.**

The product should never shame users for spending or pressure them to buy, invest, borrow, or replace a product.

## 4. Recommended tagline

**See your money. Shape your future.**

Supporting alternatives:

- Track today. Build what’s next.
- Understand today. Plan tomorrow.
- Know where it goes. Decide where you’re going.
- From everyday spending to bigger plans.

Use one primary tagline consistently at launch. The alternatives are campaign or onboarding copy, not simultaneous taglines.

## 5. Positioning statement

For individuals who want clarity about both everyday spending and future purchases, Spendova is a personal financial planning application that connects transactions, income, debt, savings, investments, owned products, and goals in one understandable view. Unlike a basic expense tracker, it explains what is affordable, what is committed, and what needs to happen next using deterministic calculations and optional privacy-controlled AI insights.

## 6. Brand pillars

### Clarity

Numbers must be understandable, traceable, and free of hidden assumptions.

### Control

The user owns the data and confirms important actions. OCR and AI produce drafts or explanations, not silent financial changes.

### Progress

The product connects daily actions with longer-term goals without guilt or unrealistic promises.

### Foresight

Spendova helps users prepare for bills, EMIs, purchases, repairs, and product replacement before they become surprises.

### Trust

Actual, projected, estimated, and AI-generated information must remain visibly distinct.

## 7. Brand personality

Spendova should feel:

- calm, not alarming;
- intelligent, not complicated;
- encouraging, not judgmental;
- modern, not speculative;
- personal, not childish;
- transparent, not overly authoritative.

## 8. Voice and language

Preferred language:

- “You have ₹12,400 available after planned commitments.”
- “At your current saving rate, this goal is projected for March 2028.”
- “Review recommended because repair costs increased this year.”
- “Here are the assumptions behind this estimate.”

Avoid:

- “You wasted too much money.”
- “You must buy this now.”
- “Guaranteed savings.”
- “Our AI knows the best decision.”
- “You are financially unhealthy.”

## 9. Visual symbol direction

The primary symbol should express **money in motion toward clarity**.

Recommended concept:

- a continuous `S` path representing the flow of money;
- two balanced curves representing money coming in and going out;
- a subtle forward or upward ending representing planning and progress;
- enough negative space to remain recognizable as a small mobile icon.

The symbol must not resemble a stock-price promise. Upward movement should communicate direction and progress, not guaranteed financial returns.

Avoid:

- dollar, rupee, euro, or other currency symbols as the main mark;
- coins, bank buildings, wallets, or piggy banks;
- literal exploding-star or supernova illustrations;
- candlestick charts;
- overly complex gradients;
- imagery suggesting lending, trading, or cryptocurrency as the primary product.

### Selected working direction

The selected working mark is the **Flowing S Path** in the midnight/aqua/mint color system. Its ribbon represents money moving through earning, spending, saving, and planning. The icon-only form is the primary mobile and PWA mark; horizontal light and dark lockups are used where the Spendova wordmark has sufficient space.

Current raster masters and web/mobile derivatives are stored in `assets/brand/spendova-final/`.

## 10. Initial color direction

- **Deep navy** — trust, stability, and readable dark surfaces.
- **Teal** — clarity, movement, and action.
- **Mint** — progress and positive supporting states.
- **Warm amber** — attention and commitments, used sparingly.
- **Neutral slate** — data-heavy surfaces and secondary information.

Income, spending, debt, warnings, and success states must use separate semantic application tokens rather than relying solely on brand colors.

## 11. Typography direction

- Use a clean geometric or humanist sans-serif.
- The wordmark should emphasize readability over novelty.
- Financial figures use tabular numerals in the product UI.
- Avoid making the word `Spend` visually dominant over `ova`; the brand must feel broader than expense tracking.

## 12. Required logo system

When logo production begins, create:

1. standalone app icon;
2. horizontal symbol and Spendova wordmark;
3. stacked lockup;
4. one-color dark version;
5. one-color light version;
6. small favicon/icon version;
7. clear-space and minimum-size guidance.

The existing Monevo image is an exploratory concept only. It is not the Spendova logo and must not be shipped as a final brand asset.

## 13. Brand-clearance gate

Before treating Spendova as the legal/public name:

- search exact and phonetically similar trademarks in relevant classes;
- search WIPO plus national/regional trademark registers for target markets;
- verify domain availability directly with an accredited registrar;
- verify Apple App Store and Google Play names;
- check major social handles;
- review confusingly similar finance, lending, investment, and budgeting brands;
- obtain professional trademark advice before a commercial launch.

Until this gate is complete, documentation should describe Spendova as the **working brand**.
